About Me

Passionate about creating exceptional audio experiences

I'm Matheus Braz, a mix and recording engineer based in Los Angeles, originally from Brazil. I work closely with artists and producers to help records feel intentional, honest, and fully realized. My approach is collaborative and instinct-driven, focusing on emotion, balance, and musicality rather than over-processing or chasing trends. I care deeply about preserving what makes a song feel alive, while helping it translate clearly and confidently across systems. Whether I'm involved early in the process or coming in at the final stages, my role is to support the vision, elevate the music, and make sure nothing gets in the way of how it's meant to feel.
